vhstack

Optimizing your terminal experience, workflow, and style with thoughtfully integrated tools.

Terminal Tools

nvimpp

Your Neovim setup: A fast, modular, productivity-oriented configuration that transforms NeoVim into a modern, flexible development tool.

tmuxpp

Terminal sessions with Tmux, perfectly organized: Optimized keybindings and smart integrations that make your terminal sessions more efficient and well-structured.

termpp

Personalized terminal environment: A tailor-made setup that combines colors, fonts, and tools seamlessly to customize your terminal experience to your liking.
